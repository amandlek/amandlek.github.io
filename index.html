Recent News

  • [March 2021] New preprint on enabling spatial generalization in visuomotor control from human demonstrations using a structured hand-eye action space.

About Me

I am a PhD candidate in the Stanford Vision and Learning Lab advised by Silvio Savarese and Fei-Fei Li. I work on building systems and algorithms to allow robots to leverage human insight for manipulation.
