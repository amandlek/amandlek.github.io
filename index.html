Recent News

  • [April 2025] We have released the DexMimicGen simulation environments and datasets and the AHA codebase.

  • [April 2025] 3 papers presented at ICLR 2025: MimicLabs (a study in collecting and using large-scale manipulation datasets), AHA (novel failure data generation and failure reasoning framework for robotic manipulation), and LAPA (unsupervised latent action based pretraining for robotic manipulation).

  • [March 2025] Check out Sim-and-Real Co-Training, a simple recipe that utilizes synthetic data generation tools (such as MimicGen) for efficient real-world robotic manipulation. This recipe was used to train the GR00T-N1 humanoid foundation model as well.

About Me

I am a research scientist at NVIDIA AI. I obtained my PhD degree from Stanford University, co-advised by Silvio Savarese and Fei-Fei Li. My research focuses on developing systems and algorithms to enable robots to learn useful manipulation tasks.
